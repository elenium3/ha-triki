# Third-party files

- `triki.glb`: the Triki 3D model from https://github.com/Flopsstuff/triki
  (`docs/public/controller/triki_texture.glb`), MIT License, Copyright (c) Flopsstuff.
  Textures resized to 1024 px WebP and the mesh quantized; see docs/live-card.md.
- `triki-cap.js`: our procedural model of the token (this project, MIT).
- `triki-three.js`: a bundle of three.js r187 (core, GLTFLoader, RoomEnvironment),
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, MIT License, Copyright 2010-2026 three.js authors.
